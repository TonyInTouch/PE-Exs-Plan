Evidence Base

Pastore et al. (2014): RCT of 40 men — 82.5% gained ejaculatory control within 12 weeks of structured PFMT at 3 sessions/week.

Pastore (2018): Retrospective study of 154 patients — 75% maintained results at 36-month follow-up.

Pastore multicentre (2022): 273 subjects across 5 years — 88% completed the protocol; 66% maintained results at 60-month follow-up.

La Pera & Nicastro (1996): Established relaxation and pelvic floor awareness as prerequisite to strengthening in PE rehabilitation.

RCT (2025): Adding diaphragmatic breathing to PFMT produced significantly improved IELT at 8 weeks and 1-year follow-up vs PFMT alone.

Journal of Sexual Medicine systematic review (2021): Combined PFMT and psychological/sex therapy outperformed either approach alone.

Masters & Johnson (1970); Kaplan (1989): Foundational sex therapy protocols underpinning masturbation-based practice exercises.

*Pastore et al. (2014) — 33 of 40 men gained ejaculatory control within 12 weeks of structured pelvic floor muscle training.
